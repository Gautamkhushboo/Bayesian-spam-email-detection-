Mathematical Foundations
Bayes' Theorem for Text Classification:
P(Spam | W) = [ P(Spam) × ∏ P(wi | Spam) ] / P(W)

Where W = {w1, w2, ..., wn} is the bag-of-words representation of the email message.

1
Prior Probability P(Class)

The baseline probability of receiving spam vs ham before reading any message text. Calculated directly from dataset frequencies: P(Spam) = 747 / 5572 ≈ 13.4%, P(Ham) = 4825 / 5572 ≈ 86.6%.

2
Feature Likelihood P(wi | Class) with Laplace Smoothing

The conditional probability that word wi appears given the message is Spam or Ham: P(wi | Class) = (count(wi, Class) + α) / (∑ count(w, Class) + α × |V|) where α = 1.0 prevents zero-probability collapse on unseen words.

3
Log-Space Arithmetic (Preventing Underflow)

Multiplying many small floating-point probabilities causes arithmetic underflow. In practice, Naive Bayes uses log-probabilities: ln P(Spam | W) ∝ ln P(Spam) + ∑ ln P(wi | Spam).
